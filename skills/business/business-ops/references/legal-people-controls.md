# Legal and people controls

Load for contract, compliance, privacy, HR, recruiting, compensation, or performance work.

## Legal review

Work from the actual text. Cite the clause behind each finding and distinguish present language, missing protection, ambiguity, and negotiated preference. Verify current jurisdiction-specific law from authoritative sources; do not rely on remembered deadlines or thresholds.

Surface action-changing terms first: parties and authority; scope and precedence; term, renewal, termination; fees, taxes, indexation, minimums, acceptance, and credits; IP ownership and licenses; confidentiality exclusions and survival; data roles, subprocessors, transfers, security, breach cooperation, deletion, and audit evidence; warranties; indemnity triggers/process; liability cap/base/period and carve-outs; insurance; service levels; change control; assignment; governing law and venue.

Never call a contract safe because clauses are customary or mutual. A standard cap can be commercially unacceptable; a mutual clause can be asymmetric in effect.

For an NDA, determine unilateral vs mutual disclosure, covered information, permitted use/recipients, exclusions, compelled disclosure, term/survival, return/destruction, residual knowledge, remedies, and hidden IP or non-solicit terms.

## Compliance

Build a traceable obligation register: authority and effective date, applicability facts, obligation, owner, control, evidence, frequency, gap, remediation, and review date. Separate confirmed applicability from assumptions. GDPR/DSGVO, GoBD, TDDDG, HIPAA, and SOX are routing signals—not permission to improvise current requirements.

## People decisions

- Evaluate job-related skills, observable behavior, outcomes, and consistent criteria. Exclude protected traits and proxies; refer adverse-impact and accommodation issues to HR/counsel.
- Define recruiting scorecards before interviews. Record evidence independently before debrief to reduce halo and conformity effects.
- Performance reviews need a period, expected level, concrete evidence, impact, and support. Do not turn personality impressions into facts or backfill evidence to fit a rating.
- Compensation comparisons require geography, currency, pay period, employment type, level, discipline, and data date. Separate base, variable, equity, benefits, and one-time pay. Do not compare total cash with base.
- Internal equity and market position answer different questions. Use compa-ratio (`salary / range midpoint`) only with a valid role/location range; label small or stale samples.
- Org planning separates roles from incumbents, recurring work from projects, and capacity constraints from performance issues. An org-chart gap alone does not prove headcount need.

Binding language, employee action, privacy determinations, and regulated filings require the appropriate qualified reviewer.
